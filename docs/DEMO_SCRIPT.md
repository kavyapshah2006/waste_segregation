# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record on the phone with Android's screen recorder, airplane mode visibly on from the first shot.
One take per scene; cut between scenes. Show the real app, no mock-ups.

**Before recording:** every item's explanation ships with the app, so scans show it instantly.
Ask one typed question once before recording, so Llama is already loaded (about 30 s) when
the Ask shots start. Close other apps, charge to 100%, note the battery level.

| # | Time | Shot | What to show | Say / caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Quick settings | Airplane mode on, Wi-Fi off; open SwachhSathi | "Everything runs on this phone. No internet." |
| 2 | 0:10 | **Scan**, PET bottle | Hold it in view; the result screen opens: photo, name, "Dispose in the Blue bin", steps | "A detector names it, a second model checks it, and the knowledge base decides the bin under India's SWM Rules 2026." |
| 3 | 0:30 | Same result | Scroll to **Why it matters** | "Llama 3.2 wrote this explanation on the phone, through ExecuTorch, and every sentence is checked against the knowledge base." |
| 4 | 0:45 | Banana peel or egg shells | "Is it one of these?" sheet → tap the right item → green bin | "When the two models don't agree, it asks instead of guessing. Food waste goes to the green bin." |
| 5 | 1:05 | Greasy cardboard or milk pouch | Card asks "Is it dirty?" → Yes → bin and steps change | "Condition matters, and when it can't tell, it asks." |
| 6 | 1:20 | AA battery, **shutter** | Tap the white shutter: full photo analysed → black bin, hazard warning | "Batteries are special-care waste: the app shows the hazard and where to drop them off." |
| 7 | 1:40 | **Ask**, Hindi | Settings → हिंदी; Ask → **Speak**: "मेरे पास पुरानी बैटरी है, इसे कहाँ डालूँ?" | "Hindi voice in and out, offline." |
| 8 | 2:05 | **Ask** | Type "What is the capital of France?" | Polite decline. "It stays on topic." |
| 9 | 2:20 | **History**, **Impact** | Scroll both | "A private record of what you sorted, a daily fact and a quiz." |
| 10 | 2:40 | **Settings** | Models list (all on the phone), "Built with Llama" | End card: SwachhSathi, repo link, "ExecuTorch on Arm". |

If an explanation was not prepared, Llama writes it live: the panel shows what it is doing ("Reading
the rules for this item…") and the bin above is already final. That is fine to show too.
