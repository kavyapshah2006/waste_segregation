# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record on the phone with screen recording on, airplane mode visibly on from the first shot. One take per scene; cut between scenes. Show the real app, no mock-ups.

| # | Time | Shot | What to show | Say / caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Phone, quick settings | Turn airplane mode on, Wi-Fi off | "Everything runs on this phone. No internet." |
| 2 | 0:10 | Table of mixed waste | Point the camera at a PET bottle, a banana peel, a battery | Each verdict card appears with its bin colour. "Detector, then the knowledge base decides the bin." |
| 3 | 0:35 | Greasy pizza box | Scan it | Card switches to the green bin and says to tear off the greasy part. "Condition matters: dirty cardboard is wet waste." |
| 4 | 0:55 | Broken CFL (or a photo of one) | Scan | Mercury clean-up steps, black bin. "Hazardous items get safety steps." |
| 5 | 1:15 | Explanation streaming | Tap "Why?" | Llama's explanation streams in; latency overlay shows time to first token. "Llama 3.2 explains, on the phone's CPU, through ExecuTorch." |
| 6 | 1:40 | Unknown object | Point at something not in the training set | "Not sure" → three suggestions → tap one. "When unsure it asks instead of guessing." |
| 7 | 2:00 | Voice in Hindi | Tap the mic, say "मेरे पास पुरानी बैटरी है, इसे कहाँ डालूँ?" | Battery card in Hindi, read aloud. "Hindi voice in and out, offline." |
| 8 | 2:25 | Off-topic | Ask "आज मौसम कैसा रहेगा?" | Polite decline. "It stays on topic." |
| 9 | 2:35 | Impact screen | Scroll | Items by stream, estimated kg diverted (labelled as an estimate), streak, today's quiz. |
| 10 | 2:50 | About screen | Show "Built with Llama" and licences | End card: repo link, "ExecuTorch on Arm". |

Before recording: charge to 100%, note the battery level at start and end, close other apps, record the phone model in `docs/DEVICE.md`.
